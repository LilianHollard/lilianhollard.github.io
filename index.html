Publications:

LeYOLO: A New Scalable and Efficient CNN Architecture for Object Detection

In this work, we presented key optimizations aimed at improving neural network efficiency for edge computing. We introduced LeNeck, a lightweight classification head that outperforms existing low-cost classifiers when paired with SSDLite, delivering higher MSCOCO accuracy with fewer parameters. We also proposed LeYOLO, a compact yet powerful alternative to tiny YOLO models, offering better accuracy-to-parameter and FLOP ratios through an optimized backbone and integration with LeNeck. Both LeNeck and LeYOLO deliver faster inference speeds compared to current low-parameter detectors, making them well-suited for mobile, embedded, and low-power devices. These contributions mark a step forward in deploying accurate, efficient object detection on the edge, and we look forward to future advancements building on this work.

May 2025: CRV - Canadian AI, Lilian Hollard, Lucas Mohimont, Nathalie Gaveau, Luiz-Angelo Steffenel

Exploring Superposition and Interference in State-of-the-Art Low-Parameter Vision Models

Our study uncovers how interference in feature maps impacts scaling in sub-1.5M parameter neural nets. We identify architectural methods to reduce interference and introduce NoDepth Bottleneck - a proof-of-concept demonstrating improved scaling on ImageNet. This lays groundwork for more interpretable, efficient models.

May 2025: CAIAC 2025, Lilian Hollard, Lucas Mohimont, Nathalie Gaveau, Luiz-Angelo Steffenel

Smart-Viticulture and Deep Learning: Challenges and Recent Developments on Yield Prediction

This chapter reviews non-destructive yield estimation methods in viticulture, focusing on deep learning for grape and berry counting. We present strategies and preliminary results for automated, edge-ready yield prediction systems tailored to Champagne vineyards-improving accuracy and usability in real-world settings.
